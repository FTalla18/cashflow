How to play

  1. Goal: be the first whose passive income (dividends, rentals, businesses) covers your living expenses. That's escaping the rat race.
  2. Your turn: roll the dice and move. Every 💰 Pay Day you land on or pass pays your paycheck: income minus taxes, savings and bills.
  3. 💡 Opportunity: pick a small deal (stocks, funds, starter homes, small businesses) or a big deal (apartment buildings, commercial property, bigger businesses). Check the cashflow: some deals lose money!
  4. Events: 🛍️ personal spending, 👶 new baby, 💛 charity (donate 10% to roll 2 dice for 3 turns), 📨 buyer offers for your property, 📉 layoffs, 📰 economic news that hits everyone.
  5. Manage money (after rolling): sell stocks or property (quick sales take a 20% hit), pay off debts to cut expenses, or take a bank loan in $1,000 steps.
  6. Deals get shared: pass on a deal and the next player can grab it. Stocks are open to everyone at the same price after someone buys.
  7. 📈 Market: prices drift every round and swing with news. Growth stocks pay no dividends, so sell them high. Dividend funds pay you to hold.
  8. 🏦 401(k): pick a contribution %. Most employers match 50% up to 6%: free money, but it's locked for retirement and doesn't count toward escaping.
  9. 💳 Credit score (300–850): on-time paychecks build it, running out of cash is a missed payment (−30), card balances over 30% of your limit hurt, and new loans cause a small dip. Better credit = cheaper mortgages and bank loans. Every deal card shows your rate.
  10. 🚀 Classic mode: escaping takes you to the Fast Track. Collect CashFlow Day (100× your monthly passive income), buy big businesses, dodge lawsuits and audits. Win by buying your dream or adding $50k/month of cashflow.
  11. Online: the host can set a turn timer. When it runs out, the game plays it safe for you (roll, small deal, pass, end turn). Disconnected players get skipped automatically.
  12. Between jobs? You get unemployment benefits for a while. Job offers show up on Opportunity tiles, and your odds of getting hired improve every turn.

Taxes use the latest IRS federal brackets (updated automatically when the IRS publishes new ones), FICA and approximate state rates. Raises come every 12 paychecks. Salaries come from BLS wage data. It's a game, not financial advice 💅
