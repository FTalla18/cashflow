How to play

  1. Goal: escape the rat race (your passive income from dividends, rentals and businesses covers your monthly living expenses), then win a dream on the Fast Track. The bars at the top show how close everyone is.
  2. Your money moves like real life: every 💰 Pay Day you land on or pass is one paycheck (every 2 weeks): pay minus taxes, 401(k) and health insurance. On the 1st of each month your bills go out (rent or mortgage, car, loans, cards, utilities, child care) and your passive income comes in.
  3. 💡 Opportunity: pick a small deal (stocks, funds, starter homes, small businesses) or a big deal (apartment buildings, commercial property, bigger businesses). Check the cashflow: some deals lose money!
  4. Events: 🛍️ personal spending, 👶 new baby, 💛 charity (donate 10% to roll 2 dice for 3 turns), 📨 buyer offers for your property, 📉 downsizing (usually a furlough, sometimes a real layoff), 📰 economic news that hits everyone.
  5. Manage money (after rolling): sell stocks (outside a matching stock card, a quick sale gets 10% below market) or property, make a principal payment of any amount on any debt or property loan (or pay it off), or take a bank loan in $1,000 steps. Paying down a rental's loan raises its monthly cashflow.
  6. 📬 Taxes: federal tax is withheld from every paycheck. At year end the game compares it with what you really owed (including investment income). The refund, or the bill, arrives in April.
  7. 💳 Credit score (300–850): bills paid on time build it, running out of cash is a missed payment (−30), card balances over 30% of your limit hurt, and new loans cause a small dip. Better credit = cheaper loans. Every deal card shows your rate.
  8. Deals get shared: pass on a deal and the next player can grab it. Stocks are open to everyone at the same price after someone buys.
  9. 📈 Market: prices move every round and swing with news. Each stock card shows its 52-week range. Growth stocks pay no dividends, so sell them high. Dividend funds pay you to hold.
  10. 🏦 401(k): pick a contribution %. Most employers match 50% up to 6%: free money, but it's locked for retirement and doesn't count toward escaping.
  11. 🚀 The Fast Track: when your passive income covers your expenses, you leave the square board for the Fast Track ring around it (everyone else keeps playing the rat race inside). You roll 2 dice there and collect a Payout Day worth 100× your monthly passive income. Buy big businesses, take moonshot bets, and dodge lawsuits, audits, crashes and bad partners. The game ends when someone lands on one of the 21 dreams (Dinner with the President, a private island, a pro sports team…) and buys it, or adds $50k/month of business cashflow. Your favorite dream costs 20% less. Sprint mode ends at the first escape instead.
  12. 💾 Saving: every move saves automatically. Your last 8 games show under Saved games on the home screen. Online games reopen with the same room code, and friends rejoin with the same name.
  13. Online: the host can set a turn timer. When it runs out, the game plays it safe for you. Disconnected players get skipped automatically.

Taxes use the latest IRS federal brackets (updated automatically), FICA and approximate state rates. Salaries come from BLS wage data. It's a game, not financial advice 💅
